About

Design as an act of care.

Background

I started in print and typography before moving into digital product work in 2018. Since then I've led design at an early-stage fintech, embedded with clinical teams at a health platform, and now guide a small design practice serving founders and scale-ups.

  • BFA in Visual Communication, 2017
  • Design lead, Northline Studio — 2023 to present
  • Mentor at ADPList — 120+ sessions

Philosophy

Restraint is a feature. Every element on a screen has to earn its place — through evidence, hierarchy and a genuine job to do. I work close to engineering, design in systems rather than screens, and treat accessibility as a baseline rather than a phase.

  • Research first, pixels second
  • Systems over one-off screens
  • Ship, measure, refine

Services

What I do, end to end.

Engagements run four to twelve weeks — from a focused research sprint to a full product design partnership.

UI Design

Interfaces built as systems — type scales, spacing rhythm, colour tokens and components that survive a real codebase.

UX Research

Interviews, usability testing, diary studies and analytics review — delivered as decisions your team can act on, not a 60-page deck.

Wireframing

Low-fidelity structure and information architecture — the cheapest place to be wrong and the fastest way to align a team.

Prototyping

Motion-accurate prototypes in Figma and code, built to be tested with real users before production work begins.

Design Systems

Token architecture, component libraries and the documentation that keeps them alive after the launch party.

Design Audits

A structured review against usability heuristics, WCAG 2.1 AA and your own brand — with a prioritised fix list.

Process

How the work actually happens.

Five stages, run in loops rather than a straight line. Every stage ends with something you can look at and argue with.

01

Discover

Stakeholder interviews, analytics and a competitive teardown. We agree on the problem before anyone opens Figma.

02

Define

Research synthesis into personas, journey maps and a single prioritised problem statement.

03

Ideate

Sketching, crazy eights and low-fidelity wireframes. Volume first, judgement second.

04

Prototype

High-fidelity flows with real motion and real content, ready for the hands of real users.

05

Validate

Usability sessions, iteration, then a documented handoff and post-launch measurement.
